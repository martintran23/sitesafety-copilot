# Voice and Narration Scripts

Source: `MASTER_PROMPT_CONTEXT.md` ("Presentation Narration Guidelines").

## Per-hazard `audio_response_script`
- Maximum 3 sentences. Short clauses. One explicit CFR cite when space allows.
- Spell digits as words if Polly misreads them in testing.

## Presentation narration (under 45 seconds)
1. Greeting (1 sentence): address the crew, name the site.
2. Primary Concern (1-2): highest-severity hazard, CFR cite, why it matters now.
3. Corrective Action (1-2): immediate physical fix, any stop-work condition.
4. Crew Sign-Off (1): confirm understanding or next check.

## Formatting
- Write for the ear: no nested clauses, URLs, emojis, or em dashes.
- At most three hazards; summarize the rest as "additional findings in the JHA PDF."
- Sync animated box callouts to the Primary Concern first, then secondary hazards if time remains.
- Tone: authoritative, concise, field-practical. No speculative medical claims.
