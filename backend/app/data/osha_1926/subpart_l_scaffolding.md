# OSHA 29 CFR 1926 Subpart L - Scaffolds

- Citation: 1926.451(g)(1)
  Requirement: Fall protection is required on scaffolds more than 10 feet above a lower level.
- Citation: 1926.451(g)(4)
  Requirement: Guardrail systems use top rails 38 to 45 inches high and midrails approximately halfway between the top rail and platform.
- Citation: 1926.451(b)(1)
  Requirement: Scaffold platforms must be fully planked or decked.
- Citation: 1926.451(c)(2)
  Requirement: Supported scaffolds require mudsills and base plates, or equivalent, as applicable.
