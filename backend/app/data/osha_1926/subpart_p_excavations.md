# OSHA 29 CFR 1926 Subpart P - Excavations

- Citation: 1926.652(a)(1)
  Requirement: Protective systems are required for trenches 5 feet or deeper unless the excavation is entirely in stable rock.
- Citation: 1926.651(c)(2)
  Requirement: Trenches 4 feet or deeper require adequate egress, with no more than 25 feet of lateral travel.
- Citation: 1926.651(j)(2)
  Requirement: Spoil piles and excavated material must be kept at least 2 feet back from the excavation edge.
