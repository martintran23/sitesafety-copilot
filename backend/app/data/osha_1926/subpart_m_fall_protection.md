# OSHA 29 CFR 1926 Subpart M - Fall Protection

- Citation: 1926.501(b)(1)
  Requirement: Unprotected sides and edges 6 feet or higher above a lower level require fall protection.
- Citation: 1926.502(d)
  Requirement: Personal fall arrest anchorages must support at least 5,000 pounds per employee attached.
- Citation: 1926.501(b)(4)
  Requirement: Floor holes and skylights must be protected with covers, guardrails, or equivalent protection.
