# OSHA 29 CFR 1926 Subparts E and K - PPE and Electrical

- Citation: 1926.100(a)
  Requirement: Hard hats must be worn where there is possible danger of head injury from impact, falling or flying objects, or electrical shock or burns.
- Citation: 1926.404(b)(1)
  Requirement: Construction-site temporary wiring and receptacle outlets must meet applicable ground-fault circuit-interrupter requirements.
