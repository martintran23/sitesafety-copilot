# OSHA 29 CFR 1926 Subpart X - Stairways and Ladders

- Citation: 1926.1053(b)(1)
  Requirement: Portable ladders used to access an upper landing must extend at least 3 feet above the landing surface.
- Citation: 1926.1053(b)(4)
  Requirement: Portable ladders must be used on stable and level surfaces and secured when necessary to prevent displacement.
